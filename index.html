New to Kreupasanam? Start here.

A simple, beginner's guide to the Marian Covenant — what it is, how it works, and what's expected of you.

1

Understand the Covenant

The Marian Covenant is a 90-day prayer commitment to Mother Mary, prayed daily at a time you choose. It isn't a ritual with fixed words — it's the discipline of showing up in prayer every single day.

2

Choose your start date

Pick any date to begin. Once you start, the covenant runs for 90 continuous days, so choose a date when you can commit fully.

3

Set a daily prayer time

Set aside about 15–20 minutes each day, ideally the same time, for the covenant prayer. Consistency matters more than length.

4

Join the live retreat

Fr. V.P. Joseph leads the Marian Covenant Retreat live every Tuesday at 11:30 AM on the Kreupasanam YouTube channel — join live or watch the replay anytime.

5

Stay consistent, restart if needed

If you miss a day, the common guidance is to restart the 90 days rather than skip ahead. Confirm this and any other specifics with the shrine office when you register.

CORE GUIDELINES TO KEEP IN MIND

  • Pray at the same time each day, as far as possible.
  • Attend Mass and confession during the period, as guided by the priests.
  • Treat the 90 days seriously — avoid skipping days without reason.
  • Keep your prayer sincere and simple — this is a discipline of trust, not a transaction.

This is a general orientation. For the exact, official steps, please register directly through kreupasanam.com or contact the shrine office.

How to register — step by step
  1. Search for the website: look up "Kreupasanam Online Covenant Prayer" on a search engine, or go directly to kreupasanam.com/en/covenantprayer.
  2. Access the portal: click through to the official online covenant prayer page.
  3. Log in: click "Submit Your Prayer Request" and sign in using your email address and password.
  4. Agree to terms: read the covenant conditions, then click "I Agree" to give your consent.
  5. Provide details: enter your contact information, phone number, and location.
  6. Submit requests: fill in your six specific prayer requests in the boxes provided, then click "Continue" to finish registering.

Once registered, you will receive two daily prayers by email to recite for 90 days.

What this service involves

This service is offered for devotees who cannot visit the shrine in person, or who need urgent intercession. Submitted intentions are included in daily Holy Masses, adorations, rosaries, and retreat prayers at the shrine.

The Covenant (Udampadi)

This service is bound to the Kreupasanam Marian Covenant. By submitting a request, you agree to observe these conditions, usually over 90 days:

  • Daily scripture & teachings: read the Bible daily and avoid the sinful behaviours listed in Galatians 5:19.
  • Fasting: observe fasting on Wednesdays or Saturdays.
  • Attending retreats: watch or participate in Kreupasanam retreats, in person or online via YouTube.
  • Witnessing & evangelisation: share your testimony or spread the covenant devotion to others once your intentions are answered.
Intentions

You may submit up to 6 prayer requests, symbolising the six stone water jars at the Wedding at Cana.

Daily prayer routine

Morning Intercessory Prayer, 5:30 AM — the Apostles' Creed, 7 Our Fathers and 7 Hail Marys, followed by the Apparition Intercessory Prayer.

"Mother, Holy Mother, The Mother of God, please have our heartfelt gratitude for all the blessings and graces received through Kreupasanam... in union with all the prayers of Kreupasanam, the intentions of this family be granted [mention your intentions] in this blessed moment. Amen."

Evening Covenant Prayer, after the evening Rosary / 8:30 PM — light a candle at home during evening prayer and recite:

  1. Ask Mother Mary for strength to fulfil the covenant for online participants, followed by 1 Our Father and 1 Hail Mary.
  2. Pray for family intentions alongside those attending Kreupasanam retreats, followed by 1 Our Father and 1 Hail Mary.
  3. Reiterate your family's commitment to the covenant, followed by 1 Our Father and 1 Hail Mary.
  4. Close with 1 Apostles' Creed, 1 Our Father, and 1 Hail Mary.

Frequently asked, beginner-friendly

Straight answers to what most first-time visitors ask us.

What exactly is the Marian Covenant?
It's a personal, 90-day daily prayer commitment to Mother Mary that began at Kreupasanam. There's no fixed script — you pray sincerely each day for the full 90 days.
How do I join online for the first time?
Go to the Online Covenant Registration card above, fill in your name, contact details, and preferred start date. You'll get a confirmation with your daily prayer schedule.
What are the retreat and Mass timings?
The Marian Covenant Retreat streams live every Tuesday at 11:30 AM on the Kreupasanam YouTube channel. Daily Mass timings can change, so please check kreupasanam.com for the current schedule.
I'm new — do I need any special preparation?
No. You don't need any prior experience or special items — just a sincere willingness to pray daily for 90 days. First-time visitors are always welcome.
